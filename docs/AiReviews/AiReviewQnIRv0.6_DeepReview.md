## Summary

This technical dossier presents the Loop-5 review record and candidate specifications for the Qn computational universe, covering the constitutional constraints (`SIMEMP` v1.4.1), the two-stage Best of Choices (`BOC` v0.4.2) decision protocol, six foundational domain substrates (`Qm`, `Qs`, `Qlog`, `Qphy`, `Qchem`, `Qbio`), the `Qn` Primitive Envelope (v1.4.1), the `QnIR` v0.3.0-draft.36 intermediate representation (including Step 6.1 Persistent Containers under `qir-enc-3` and Step 6.2 Bounded Acyclic Calls under `qir-enc-4`), and the Milestone 2 `QnLang` Rung-0 surface language candidate (`v0.1.0-draft.1`). Grounded in empirical Layer-0 hardware benchmarks (`QO-17`) and differential execution across independent Rust and Python reference implementations, the dossier seeks reviewer adjudication on three primary decision requests—the ordering of Step 6.3 / Stage 1 prerequisites versus the Stage 0 compiler seed, verification of the container and call extensions, and validation of the `QnLang` Rung-0 zero-concession grammar—yielding a recommended disposition of **`REVISE AND RESUBMIT`** strictly due to dossier text-extraction truncations in the reproduced `QnLang` EBNF grammar and Markdown tables, while confirming the byte-identical validity of the executed `QnIR` core, container (`--fixture C`), and call (`--fixture K`) artifacts.

## Strengths

* **Rigorous Architectural Synthesis and Deterministic Semantics:** The architecture cohesively integrates exact arbitrary-precision integer and normalized odd-mantissa dyadic arithmetic over a fixed-width 64-bit word transport, basic-block-with-arguments SSA semantics, a two-tier operational and semantic status token lifecycle, and a single reserve/charge/refund work-accounting ledger (`Work(E)`).
* **Sound Compositional Budget Admission and Sub-Budget Conservation:** The Step 6.1 and Step 6.2 extensions cleanly incorporate persistent copy-on-write containers (`ARRAY_RRB16` and `TABLE_MBTREE16`), hash-chained call frames (`stack_root_{d+1}`) that keep live `P2` configuration state proportional to the top frame $O(F_{\text{top}})$, and context-independent compositional `B_hat` summaries that preserve the soundness inequality `B_hat(P) = B_cfg(P) >= B_sem(P)` and exact escrow conservation across all 270 benchmarked execution rows and fatal callee rejections.
* **Distinctive Zero-Concession Surface Language Co-Design:** `QnLang` Rung-0 thoughtfully unifies content-addressed De Bruijn expression DAGs (`CAE-AST`), an optional provenance-only `NameMap` sidecar, affine container ownership with persistent fallbacks, and explicit cross-domain type and epistemic-modality gates (`Qm`, `Qs`, `Qlog`, `Qphy`, `Qchem`, `Qbio`) that enforce operator hygiene (`=` confined to exact equality) and strict `F19` separation from `src/qnir/`.
* **Transparent Differential Verification and Empirical Honesty:** The dossier demonstrates admirable scientific rigor by pairing every retained `QnIR` extension with byte-identical Rust and Python differential fixture transcripts (`--fixture C` digest `2c96b152f38a7d44` and `--fixture K` digest `e83189cb00a56497`) and candidly documenting negative empirical findings, such as the `F14` gap showing that logical work `U` orders computation but does not uniformly bound physical wall-clock time.

## Weaknesses

* **Truncated EBNF Grammar and Stripped Normative Tables in the Assembled Dossier:** Due to line-filtering and Markdown pipe-table stripping during dossier assembly, the reproduced `QnLang` Rung-0 EBNF block (§II.14, pp. 93–94) drops multiple alternation branches and rule headers—rendering it unable to derive the five worked reference programs in §6.4—and omits essential normative tables across `boc.md`, `qs.md`, `qnPrimitive.md`, `qnir_semantics.md`, `qnir_containers.md`, `qnir_calls.md`, `qnlang.md`, and the §III.2 benchmark logs, which under the dossier's own §IV.3 rules triggers a `REVISE AND RESUBMIT` disposition until the missing productions and tables are restored.
* **Algorithmic Scalability of Path-Unrolling Admission and Per-Step State-Root Hashing Compared to Prior Art:** Computing the static admission surrogate `B_hat(P)` via explicit trip-ceiling state unrolling suffers from exponential state-space blowup in the number of intra-procedural back-edges ($|B| \prod (\text{ceil}_i + 1) \le S_{\text{max}} = 4096$), and committing full `P1 = SHA-256(P2)` CBOR state roots at every single instruction step imposes substantial runtime overhead (~4,500 cycles/U and up to +171%–336% call overhead at depth $C=8$); the authors might consider contextualizing and comparing these design choices against polynomial-time Implicit Path Enumeration (IPET) WCET analysis and established fuel-metered or panic-free verifiable IRs (Reference Paper: The Worst-Case Execution-Time Problem—Overview of Methods and Survey of Tools, Reinhard Wilhelm et al., 2008; Reference Paper: Bringing the Web up to Speed with WebAssembly, Andreas Haas et al., 2017; Reference Paper: Cairo 1.0 & Sierra: Safe Intermediate Representation for Provable Computation, Lior Goldberg et al., 2023).
* **Pre-Calibration Denial-of-Service Asymmetries in Structural Validation and Wide-Word Arithmetic:** Before Layer-0 calibration (`QO-17`) is completed, executing an unguarded $O(|B|^3)$ iterative dominator pass (`34.5 µs` on 64 blocks) prior to checking the `S_max = 4096` gate—combined with billing a flat `4 U` for `MUL`/`DIV` while multi-word schoolbook division scales as $O(n^2)$ (22.4× slower at 16 words)—leaves a potential computational asymmetry against static gateway and instruction fees unless pre-dominator block/edge caps and operand-width-parameterized cost formulas are enforced.
* **Cross-Specification Alignment Gaps and Templated Review Appendices:** The canonical baseline contains a zoom-exponent sign inversion at the `Qm` $\to$ `QnIR` `ZOOM=4` lowering boundary ($2^{-z}$ in `qm.md` §4.3 vs. $m \cdot 2^z$ in `qnir_semantics.md` §2.3), an unhandled boundary case in `qm.md` §6's interval ordering $\mathrm{Rel}(A,B)$ at shared open/closed endpoints, abbreviated instruction listings in fixtures `K3`–`K5`, and 52 programmatically cycled Review Appendices (`R-001`–`R-052`) that introduce anachronistic commit pairings and fabricated cross-file dependencies solely to meet a BPE token-padding target.

### [Foundational Domain Specifications (Qm, Qs, Qlog, Qphy, Qchem, Qbio) and Qn Primitive Envelope] (Pages: 22-69)

1. **Zoom-Sign Inversion and Normalization Mismatch at the `Qm` $\to$ `QnIR` Lowering Boundary (`qm.md` §4.3, p. 27 vs. `qnir_semantics.md` §2.3 & §2.6, pp. 72, 75)**
   - **Location:** Section II.3 (`qm.md` v1.4.1), §3.3 (p. 24) and §4.3 ("Zoom sign and QnIR lowering [v1.4.1]", p. 27); cf. Section II.10 (`qnir_semantics.md` v0.3.0-draft.8.4), §2.3 (p. 72) and §2.6 (p. 75).
   - **Observation:** In `qm.md` §3.3 (p. 24), a declared zoom level $z \in \mathbb{N}$ parameterizes a rational subdivision quantum $\delta_z(d) = Q(1)_d / \kappa(z)$, so under the `Dyadic` schedule ($\kappa(z) = 2^z$), $\delta_z = 2^{-z}$ and a terminal-exact tuple $\langle \sigma, q_z, z, Q(0), d \rangle$ denotes $\sigma \cdot q_z \cdot 2^{-z}$. In `qm.md` §4.3 ("Zoom sign and QnIR lowering [v1.4.1]", p. 27), the specification states:
     > *"The convention `δ_z = Q(1)/κ(z)` fixes `δ_z` as a **positive** scale subdivision at zoom `z`. QnIR's binary `ZOOM=4` word carries the exponent `z` with effective scale `2^-z`; for the `Dyadic` schedule the two agree exactly (`δ_z = 2^-z`), so a dyadic leaf lowers directly to a binary `ZOOM=4` word with the same exponent."*

     However, in the owning QnIR operational semantics (`qnir_semantics.md` §2.3, p. 72; §2.6, p. 75), a zoom-carrying `ZOOM=4` value $(m, z_{\mathrm{qnir}})$ is defined as denoting *"the exact dyadic quantity `m·2^z`"* (with a positive multiplier power $2^{z_{\mathrm{qnir}}}$ rather than $2^{-z_{\mathrm{qnir}}}$), normalizes $m = 2^k \cdot q$ (with $q$ odd) via $N(m, z_{\mathrm{qnir}}) = (q, z_{\mathrm{qnir}} + k)$, and orders normalized pairs $(q_1, z_1)$ and $(q_2, z_2)$ for $z_1 \le z_2$ by comparing $q_1$ against $q_2 \cdot 2^{z_2 - z_1}$. Furthermore, `qnir_semantics.md` §2.6 (p. 75) mandates that a `ZOOM=4` value be encoded in the normalized odd-mantissa form of $N$ and that *"a non-normalized zoom encoding is malformed."*
   - **Potential Concern:** This represents a definitive cross-specification contradiction at the lowering boundary. Under QnIR's definition, factoring out $2^k$ from the mantissa *increments* the exponent ($z_{\mathrm{qnir}} + k$), whereas in `qm.md` §4.3 dividing $q_z$ by $\rho(z) = 2$ during `z_min` reduction *decrements* the zoom level ($z \leftarrow z - 1$). Lowering a `Qm` dyadic leaf with $z > 0$ to a QnIR `ZOOM=4` word "with the same exponent" ($+z$) inverts the scale factor from $2^{-z}$ to $2^{+z}$ (a factor of $2^{2z}$ error). To denote the same dyadic value under `qnir_semantics.md` §2.3/§2.6, a `Qm` dyadic leaf reduced to $z_{\min}$ must lower to a QnIR `ZOOM=4` pair with a **negated** exponent $z_{\mathrm{qnir}} = -z_{\min}$ (encoded with `INT_NEG` for $z_{\min} > 0$) and odd mantissa $\sigma \cdot q_{z_{\min}}$, or the exponent sign convention between `qm.md` §4.3 and `qnir_semantics.md` §2.3 must be explicitly reconciled.

2. **Incompleteness of the Positional Ordering Relation $\mathrm{Rel}(A, B)$ at Half-Open Interval Boundaries and Handle Opacity (`qm.md` §6, p. 32)**
   - **Location:** Section II.3 (`qm.md` v1.4.1), §6 ("Relational Decidability and Positional Ordering"), p. 32.
   - **Observation:** In `qm.md` §6 (p. 32), the sign-aware interval enclosure for a tuple $A = \langle \sigma_A, q_A, z, r_A, d \rangle$ carrying an unexpanded residual commitment handle (§3.4, Form 2) is defined over half-open intervals:
     $$I_z(A) = \begin{cases} \big[\, q_A \cdot \delta_z, \; (q_A + 1) \cdot \delta_z \big) & \text{if } \sigma_A = \oplus, \; r_A \neq Q(0) \\ \big( -(q_A + 1) \cdot \delta_z, \; -q_A \cdot \delta_z \big] & \text{if } \sigma_A = \ominus, \; r_A \neq Q(0) \\ \big[\, Q(0), \; Q(0) \,\big] & \text{if } \sigma_A = \odot \end{cases}$$

     and the positional ordering relation $\mathrm{Rel}(A, B)$ is defined by four cases:
     $$\mathrm{Rel}(A, B) = \begin{cases} A \lt B & \text{if } \sup I_z(A) \lt \inf I_z(B) \\ A \gt B & \text{if } \inf I_z(A) \gt \sup I_z(B) \\ A \equiv B & \text{if both residuals collapsed and } \mathcal{H}(A) = \mathcal{H}(B) \text{ after §4.3 normalization} \\ \mathrm{Indeterminate} & \text{if } I_z(A) \cap I_z(B) \neq \emptyset \text{ and at least one residual is an unexpanded handle} \end{cases}$$
   - **Potential Concern:** There are two boundary issues in this formulation:
     1. *Non-exhaustiveness at shared open/closed boundary endpoints (Definitive Mathematical Gap):* Suppose $A$ is a Form-2 tuple with $\sigma_A = \oplus$ and $r_A \neq Q(0)$, yielding $I_z(A) = [q_A \cdot \delta_z, (q_A + 1) \cdot \delta_z)$, and suppose $B$ is an exact collapsed-residual tuple (Form 1) at the upper endpoint $(q_A + 1) \cdot \delta_z$, yielding the point interval $I_z(B) = [(q_A + 1) \cdot \delta_z, (q_A + 1) \cdot \delta_z]$ (or another Form-2 tuple with $I_z(B) = [(q_A + 1) \cdot \delta_z, (q_A + 2) \cdot \delta_z)$). Every element of $I_z(A)$ is strictly less than every element of $I_z(B)$, and their set intersection is empty ($I_z(A) \cap I_z(B) = \emptyset$). However, because $\sup I_z(A) = (q_A + 1) \cdot \delta_z = \inf I_z(B)$, the strict inequality $\sup I_z(A) \lt \inf I_z(B)$ is **false**, while the $\mathrm{Indeterminate}$ condition $I_z(A) \cap I_z(B) \neq \emptyset$ is **also false**—leaving $\mathrm{Rel}(A, B)$ undefined at shared boundary endpoints. 3. **Potential Specification Ambiguity in `OP_CALL` Binary Word-Count Formula (`qnir_calls.md` §8.3, p. 88)**
