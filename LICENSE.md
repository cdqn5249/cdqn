### 1.5 "Scale" / "Scaling Usage"

"Scale" / "Scaling Usage" means any use of the Licensed Work or Derivative Works by an entity (including its affiliates) that meets or exceeds ANY of the following threshold criteria ("Scaling Threshold"):

- **c) Deployment Scale:** More than 10,000 concurrent active compute nodes, containers, instances, or active autonomous agents running the Licensed Work or a Derivative Work.
- **d) User Scale:** More than 100,000 Monthly Active Users (MAU) or more than 10,000,000 API/Qn transactions per month processed by the entity's use of the Licensed Work or a Derivative Work.

Scaling Usage is measured against entities that deploy, operate, compile, bind, distribute, or otherwise directly use the Licensed Work or Derivative Works.
